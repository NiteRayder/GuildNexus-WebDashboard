This Privacy Policy explains what information NyxEclipse may process, why it is used, and how users can request deletion of stored data. NyxEclipse is a Discord bot developed and maintained by NyxNexus Development.

  • Identification Data: User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, and Channel IDs required to operate Discord features.

NyxEclipse does not intentionally maintain a permanent archive of raw Discord message content for ordinary bot operation.
