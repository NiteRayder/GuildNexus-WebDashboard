NyxEclipse Terms & Policy

Effective Date: July 19, 2026

1. Introduction

These Terms of Service (“Terms”) govern your use of NyxEclipse, a Discord bot developed and maintained by NyxNexus Development. By adding, using, or interacting with NyxEclipse, you agree to these Terms and the Privacy Policy contained herein. If you do not agree, you must remove the bot from your server and discontinue use.

2. Eligibility & Requirements

To use NyxEclipse, you must:

  • Be at least 13 years old (Discord's minimum age requirement).
  • Follow all Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • Have the necessary permissions ("Manage Server" or Administrator) to add bots to a server.

3. AI-Powered Features

NyxEclipse includes AI-driven systems for moderation, content generation, and automated decision-making.

  • Accuracy: AI outputs may be imperfect, incomplete, or incorrect.
  • Staff Overrides: AI moderation decisions may be automated but can be overridden by server staff.
  • No Professional Advice: AI features should not be treated as professional advice (legal, medical, financial, etc.).

4. User Responsibilities & Prohibitions

You agree to use NyxEclipse in compliance with Discord's rules and applicable laws. You may not use NyxEclipse to automate spam or raids, collect data unlawfully, circumvent Discord moderation, or generate harmful content.

5. Integrated Privacy Policy & Data Handling

Information Collected

  • Identification Data: User IDs, Server (Guild) IDs, and Channel IDs.
  • Configuration: Server-specific configurations, prefix preferences, and feature toggles.
  • Logs: Command execution metrics, error logs, and moderation event history.
  • Message Content: NyxEclipse does not store raw message content. Messages processed for AI features are held ephemerally in memory during execution and deleted immediately.

Data Deletion Requests

Server owners or users can request deletion of stored data associated with their Server ID or User ID by contacting support.
